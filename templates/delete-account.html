KinGuard — Delete Your Account

Last updated: 15 August 2026

This page explains how to delete your KinGuard account and all of the data associated with it. KinGuard is an independent project; you can reach us at kinguard.support@gmail.com.

Delete your account from inside the app

Deletion takes effect immediately and does not need to go through us.

  1. Open KinGuard and log in as the family member.
  2. Go to the Setup screen.
  3. Scroll to the bottom, to the section headed Account.
  4. Tap Delete my account.
  5. Read the confirmation, enter your password, and tap Delete forever.

You will be signed out straight away and returned to the login screen.

Before you delete: this also switches off the elderly person’s phone. Their KinGuard link is revoked, so their SOS button will no longer be able to reach you. Their phone will show a message telling them it is no longer connected.

What is deleted

Everything tied to the account is removed from our database immediately. Nothing is archived, and the account is not merely deactivated or frozen. Specifically:

  • Your login — email address and password hash.
  • The setup details — the elder’s name and phone number, the family member’s name and phone number, accessibility needs, and language preference.
  • The entire alert history for the account.
  • The device link on the elder’s phone, which is revoked and deleted.
  • Any push notification subscriptions you had enabled.

We keep no residual copy of any of it. Deletion cannot be undone.

If you cannot log in

KinGuard does not currently send email, so there is no automatic password reset. If you have forgotten your password and cannot get into the app, email kinguard.support@gmail.com from the address you registered with and ask us to delete your account. We will verify the request and delete it for you.

Please send the request from the email address the account was registered with — that is how we confirm the account is yours.

Removing the app itself

Deleting your account removes your data from our servers. Uninstalling the app from a phone is separate, and can be done at any time from the phone’s home screen in the usual way. Uninstalling on its own does not delete your account.

Questions

KinGuard — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 August 2026

KinGuard (“the app,” “we,” “us”) helps families protect elderly relatives from scams. A family member creates an account and links a senior’s phone; if the senior taps a warning, the family member receives an alert. This policy explains, in plain language, what information the app handles and how we protect it. We have tried to collect as little as possible.

Who runs KinGuard

KinGuard is an independent project. For any privacy question or request, contact us at kinguard.support@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We only collect what the app needs to work. Specifically:

  • Family account: your email address and a password. Passwords are never stored in readable form — only a secure one-way hash is kept.
  • Setup details: the senior’s name and phone number, the family member’s name and phone number, the senior’s accessibility needs (for example larger text, vibration, or read-aloud), and a language preference. Phone numbers are stored in encrypted form.
  • Alerts: when a senior taps a warning, we store which type of situation it was and the time it happened. Alerts do not contain phone numbers or message content.
  • Notifications (optional): if you turn on push notifications, we store the technical subscription your browser provides so alerts can reach you when the app is closed.
  • Sign-in and device cookies: a login session cookie for the family member, a security token, and a device token on the senior’s phone so it stays linked without logging in.
  • Abuse prevention: we may briefly process your network (IP) address in memory to limit repeated failed login attempts. This is not stored in our database.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • We do not show ads and do not use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We do not read your text messages, contacts, call history, or other apps. The app only knows what a user types into it or taps within it.
  • We do not record calls or collect the phone numbers of scammers or third parties.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your family account.
  • To link a senior’s phone to your account and show the right accessibility settings.
  • To deliver an alert (and coaching on what to say) to the family member when the senior taps a warning.
  • To place a phone call from your own phone when you tap a call button — the number is only used to start the call on your device.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

How we protect your information

  • Phone numbers are encrypted where they are stored.
  • Passwords are hashed, never kept in readable form.
  • The app is served over HTTPS (an encrypted connection).
  • Each family’s data is isolated — one account cannot see or change another family’s information.
  • The link on a senior’s phone uses a random, revocable token that the family member can reset at any time.

No online service can promise perfect security, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Who your information is shared with

We keep sharing to the minimum needed to run the app:

  • Our hosting provider stores the app’s data on its servers on our behalf.
  • Your browser’s notification service (for example Google, Mozilla, or Apple) is used only if you enable push notifications, to deliver the alert to your device.

We do not share your information with anyone else, except where required by law.

How long we keep your information

We keep your account and setup details for as long as you use the app. Alerts are short-lived. When you delete your account, everything listed above is removed from our database immediately — we keep no residual copy, and the account is not merely deactivated or frozen.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all of its data yourself, from inside the app, without contacting us:

  • Open the Setup screen and scroll to the section headed Account.
  • Tap Delete my account, read the confirmation, and enter your password.

This removes your login, the setup details, the whole alert history, the link on the elder’s phone and any notification subscriptions. It cannot be undone, and it also stops the elder’s phone from being able to reach you. Full instructions are on our account deletion page.

If you have forgotten your password and cannot get into the app, email kinguard.support@gmail.com from the address you registered with and we will delete it for you.

Your choices and rights

  • View or change your setup details any time from within the app.
  • Unlink a senior’s phone by resetting its link from Setup.
  • Delete your data yourself from the app — see above.
  • Turn off notifications at any time.

Children’s privacy

KinGuard is intended for adults setting up protection for elderly family members. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Because the app currently sends no email, password reset is handled manually — contact us at the address above if you are locked out.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy: kinguard.support@gmail.com.
